About

Moris Dakar is a Syrian pharmacist, documentary filmmaker, YouTube content creator, and video producer based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

His work combines cinematic storytelling, marketing strategy, and real-world production experience across documentaries, events, brand content, YouTube videos, and social media campaigns.

Today, Moris Dakar Productions works with a trusted creative team to help businesses, brands, and individuals transform ideas into polished visual stories.
